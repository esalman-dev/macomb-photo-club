Welcome to the Macomb Online Photo Club

Welcome to the Macomb Online Photo Club, a vibrant, community-driven space dedicated to celebrating the art of photography in all its forms. Whether you are a seasoned professional with decades behind the lens or a curious beginner who just picked up your first camera, this is the place for you. Our club was founded on the belief that photography is one of the most powerful and accessible forms of creative expression available to us today. Every month, our members submit their finest work, vote for their favorites, and celebrate the winners in our permanent online gallery. We are thrilled that you have found us, and we warmly invite you to become a part of our growing community of visual storytellers right here in Macomb and beyond.

The Macomb Photo Club has been bringing together photography enthusiasts since 2026. Over the years, we have hosted hundreds of themed contests, showcased thousands of incredible images, and helped countless photographers improve their skills through friendly competition and peer feedback. Our three main contest categories — Nature & Landscape, Urban & Street, and Portrait & People — rotate monthly, giving every member the chance to shine no matter what their specialty may be. Participation is completely free. All you need to get started is a passion for photography and a photo worth sharing. We look forward to seeing the world through your eyes. Feel free to reach out to us at any time using the email link in the sidebar to your right.
